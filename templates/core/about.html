About us

Helping 2 lakh+ Bangladeshi sellers own their online business.

We're building the AI shop manager for F-commerce: a store that sets itself up from a photo, takes orders while you sleep, and keeps fake COD orders out.

Our mission

Every seller deserves a store they own.

65% of digital commerce in Bangladesh comes from F-commerce sellers — yet there are only about 2,000 dedicated e-commerce sites against 3 lakh+ Facebook selling pages. When Facebook was restricted in 2024, many of them lost sales overnight.

We want every one of those sellers to have their own brand, their own customers and their own data — without needing a developer, a designer or a copywriter.

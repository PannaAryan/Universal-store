Uptime SLA

We commit to 99.9% monthly uptime for storefronts and checkout, measured on our public status page.

  • Below 99.9% (more than ~43 minutes of downtime): 10% credit on next month's bill.
  • Below 99.5%: 25% credit.
  • Below 99.0%: 50% credit.

Credits are applied automatically. Scheduled maintenance announced 48 hours ahead and nationwide internet shutdowns are excluded.

Refunds

  • Monthly plans can be cancelled any time; you keep access until the end of the paid period.
  • Annual plans can be refunded pro-rata within the first 30 days.
  • Unused add-on credits (SMS, AI) are non-refundable but never expire while your account is active.
